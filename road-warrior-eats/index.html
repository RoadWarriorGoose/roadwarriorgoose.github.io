Road Warrior Eats — Privacy Policy

Road Warrior Eats — Privacy Policy

App: Road Warrior Eats (iPhone and Android)

Developer: Aaron Jason Fox (individual developer)

Contact: descendantsopco@gmail.com

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Road Warrior Eats ("the app") shows nutrition figures copied from restaurant chains' published nutrition guides. This policy covers what leaves your phone and why. The app has no account and never asks for your name, email or phone number.

On your phone only

These stay in the app's local storage and are never sent to the developer:

  • starred items, saved meal builds, the compare list, filters and protein preferences
  • your daily macro targets and the Today log (what you logged, kept for 14 days)

Deleting the app deletes them.

Location (optional)

Find a meal and Favorites nearby can use your location to find chains with a restaurant near you. The app asks only when you turn on Nearby. When you do, the coordinates you just used and the search radius are sent to an OpenStreetMap Overpass server (overpass-api.de, z.overpass-api.de or overpass.openstreetmap.fr) to look up nearby restaurants. No account id is sent. The answer is kept in memory for about 10 minutes. Location is not used for ads by the app, and it is not sold. Opening a restaurant in Maps hands its coordinates to Apple Maps or Google Maps.

Ads (free plan only)

The free plan shows one small banner ad from Google AdMob. Pro has no ads. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; on Android, the Android advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in Settings)
  • IP address (which can suggest an approximate location), device and app information
  • ad interactions (views, taps) and diagnostics such as crashes and performance

On iPhone, if you don't allow tracking, ads are non-personalized. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, a Google consent message lets you choose. You can change your choice later in About > Ad privacy choices when that option applies. Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Pro)

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The developer never sees your card details. The app uses RevenueCat to check whether you have Pro. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user id, your purchase receipts (product, price, dates, renewal status), and basic device information (model, OS version, country/storefront). It does not receive your name or email from the app. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell personal information.
  • It does not collect health records, your weight, or contacts.
  • It does not use analytics SDKs other than the ad SDK described above.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to content rated PG or lower.

Your choices and rights

You can deny location, decline tracking on iPhone, change your ad consent, reset your advertising ID, or delete the app. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or California law), you may have rights to access or delete data. Because the developer holds no account data, contact Google (AdMob) or RevenueCat for data they process, or email the contact above and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
